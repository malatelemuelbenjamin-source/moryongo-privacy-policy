Account Deletion

Learn how to request deletion of your MoryonGo account and associated personal information.

You can request deletion even if you no longer have access to the MoryonGo app. Use the contact method below and send your request from the email address associated with your account, where possible.

How to request account deletion

  1. Tap the email link below to contact the MoryonGo privacy team.
  2. Use the subject line MoryonGo Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your MoryonGo account. Do not send your password.
  4. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account before processing the request.
Email an account deletion request

What will be deleted

After we verify and approve a valid request, we will delete or arrange deletion of the MoryonGo account and associated personal information held in our active systems, including account authentication records and profile information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Information that is not linked to an identifiable user, or that must be retained to comply with legal obligations or resolve security and fraud issues, may be retained where permitted by law. Any retained information will be limited to the applicable purpose and retention period.

How long does deletion take?

We will review and process verified requests within the period required by applicable law. If additional verification or information is needed, we will contact you. Data held in backups may remain temporarily until those backups are overwritten or securely deleted under the applicable backup retention schedule.

Important information

  • Uninstalling MoryonGo does not delete your account.
  • Do not include your password or other login credentials in your request.
  • Deleting your account may permanently remove access to your profile and account-related features.
