# PRIVACY POLICY ## MoryonGo **Effective Date:** November 13, 2026 **Last Updated:** November 13, 2026 ### 1. Introduction Welcome to MoryonGo. MoryonGo is a mobile application designed to provide commuters with access to transportation-related information and services. This Privacy Policy explains how MoryonGo ("we," "our," or "us") collects, accesses, uses, stores, protects, and shares personal information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with applicable Philippine data protection laws, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). By using MoryonGo, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. ### 2. Information We Collect Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, MoryonGo may collect the following information: **A. Account and Authentication Information** - Email address - Account authentication information - User ID - Account registration and login information Account authentication is handled through Supabase Authentication. We do not intend to collect or store your plaintext password. **B. User Profile Information** When you create or update your profile, we may collect: - Full name or display name - Profile photograph, if provided - Other profile information voluntarily submitted through the application **C. Location Information** MoryonGo may request access to your device's location through GPS or Android location services. Location information may include: - Approximate location - Precise GPS coordinates - Current location, when you use location-based features We use location information to support relevant transportation, navigation, commuter, and location-based features. Location access depends on your device permissions. You can manage or revoke location permissions through your Android device settings. MoryonGo does not intentionally collect background location information unless a separately disclosed feature requires it and the appropriate permission has been obtained. **D. Notification Information** If you enable notifications, MoryonGo may process: - Notification preferences - Device notification tokens or identifiers required for delivery - Notification delivery information Notifications may be used to provide application updates, service announcements, and relevant information. You can disable notifications through your device settings. **E. Technical and Device Information** When you access the application, certain technical information may be processed by the application or its service providers, including: - Device type and operating system version - Application version - IP address and connection information - Technical logs and diagnostic information, where enabled We use this information for application operation, security, troubleshooting, and service improvement. ### 3. How We Use Your Information We may use collected information for the following purposes: - Creating and managing user accounts. - Authenticating users and securing account access. - Managing user profiles. - Providing transportation and commuter-related features. - Supporting GPS-based and location-related services. - Sending notifications when enabled. - Maintaining and improving application functionality. - Detecting suspicious activity and preventing unauthorized access. - Responding to user inquiries and support requests. - Complying with applicable legal obligations. We only intend to use personal information for legitimate purposes related to MoryonGo and its disclosed features. ### 4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services MoryonGo uses Supabase as its backend service provider. Supabase may provide: - Authentication and account management. - Database storage and processing. - Backend infrastructure. - File storage, if enabled by the application. Information submitted through MoryonGo may be transmitted to and stored using Supabase infrastructure. Supabase's official privacy information is available at: https://supabase.com/privacy Depending on the notification implementation, MoryonGo may also use Google or another notification delivery provider to deliver push notifications. These providers may process information necessary to operate their services. We do not sell personal information to third parties. We do not authorize third parties to use personal information for purposes unrelated to providing their services to MoryonGo. We may disclose information when required by applicable law, valid legal process, or to protect the rights and security of users and the application. ### 5. Data Security We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against: - Unauthorized access - Unauthorized disclosure - Loss or destruction - Misuse or alteration Security measures may include encrypted network connections, access controls, authentication protections, and database security configurations. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. ### 6. Data Retention We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide MoryonGo services, maintain user accounts, fulfill legitimate operational requirements, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Information remaining in backups may be retained temporarily until those backups are overwritten or securely deleted according to the applicable backup retention schedule. ### 7.
